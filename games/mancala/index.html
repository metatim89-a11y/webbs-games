PLAYER 1 WINS!
Objective: Collect the most stones in your store.

Rules:
  • Tap one of your pits to pick up its stones and drop one in each pit, going counter-clockwise.
  • You drop stones in your own store, but skip the other player's store.
  • If your last stone lands in your store, you get another turn.
  • If it lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture it and the stones in the pit opposite.
  • When one side runs out, each player keeps the stones left on their own side. Most stones wins.
