Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026

The short version: Webbs Games is a private game room for one family. No ads, nothing for sale, and nobody's information is sold or shared with strangers. Tim and Cassie can read messages to keep everyone safe.

What we keep

  • Your profile: the name you pick, your favourite animal, favourite game and theme colours.
  • Your PIN: used to sign you in. It is turned into a password that only the sign-in service checks; nobody, including Tim, can look it up.
  • Messages: private messages between family members and the family chat.
  • Games: wins and losses, open game tables, and game invites.

We do not ask for email addresses, phone numbers, birthdays, photos, locations or real last names.

Who can see it

  • Only people Tim or Cassie have approved can see family names, messages, the lobby and invites.
  • A private message can be read by the two people in it, and by Tim and Cassie (the admins), who can delete anything unkind.
  • Nobody can change or delete a message after sending it, or send as someone else.

Where it is stored

  • Accounts and messages are stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud) in the United States.
  • Online games connect two phones directly using the free PeerJS service, which only helps the phones find each other.
  • Some settings (sounds, saved players) stay on your own phone.

Removing your information

Ask Tim or Cassie and they will delete your account, profile, messages and game history. Admins can also delete single messages at any time.

Kids

Children play here with a parent's OK: a parent approves every account before it can message anyone, and parents can read and remove messages.
